# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the members and contributors of the rust-wasmpack-loader project, and its maintainer, pledge to make participation in this project free of harassment for every person. This applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep the project open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy.

## Our standards

These are examples of behavior that makes the project a good place to work:

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect opinions, viewpoints, and experiences that differ from your own.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes. Apologize to the people they affected, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the whole project, not only for yourself.

These are examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing the private information of another person, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer makes the standards of acceptable behavior clear and enforces them. The maintainer takes fair corrective action in response to any behavior that the maintainer considers inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer has the right and the responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. When it is appropriate, the maintainer gives the reasons for a moderation decision.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces. These are the GitHub repository [yeskiy/rustwasm-loader](https://github.com/yeskiy/rustwasm-loader), with its issues, pull requests, discussions, and security advisory threads.

It also applies when a person represents the project in any other place. Examples are a post from an official project account, or an appearance as the representative of the project at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or other unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at [eqwer24@outlook.com](mailto:eqwer24@outlook.com). The maintainer reviews and investigates every complaint fairly.

The maintainer keeps the identity of the reporter private, and respects the privacy and security of every person who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer uses these guidelines to decide the consequence of each action that violates this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Impact on the project**: Inappropriate language, or other behavior that the maintainer considers unprofessional or unwelcome in the project.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from the maintainer. The warning explains the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. The maintainer can ask for a public apology.

### 2. Warning

**Impact on the project**: A violation through one incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences if the behavior continues. For a specified period, the person must not interact with the people involved. This includes unsolicited interaction with the maintainer, who enforces this Code of Conduct. It applies in project spaces and in external channels, such as social media. If the person does not obey these terms, the maintainer can apply a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact on the project**: A serious violation of the project standards, including inappropriate behavior that continues over time.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from all interaction and public communication with the project for a specified period. During this period, the person must not interact with the people involved, in public or in private. This includes unsolicited interaction with the maintainer, who enforces this Code of Conduct. If the person does not obey these terms, the maintainer can apply a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact on the project**: A pattern of violations of the project standards. This includes inappropriate behavior that continues over time, harassment of a person, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from all public interaction in the project.
